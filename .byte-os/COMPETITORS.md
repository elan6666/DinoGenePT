# Comparators

| Product | Positioning | Role here | Strength | Weakness | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GenePT Ada | NCBI text gene embedding | Paper reference | Published protocol | Older model and NCBI-only text | [GitHub](https://github.com/yiqunchen/GenePT) |
| GenePT model-3 | NCBI + UniProt text embedding | Latest official baseline | Same richer source text | 3072/native dimension may confound | [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/10833191) |
| Gene2vec | Expression-derived gene vectors | GGI contextual baseline | Original fixed benchmark | Different information source | [GitHub](https://github.com/jingcheng-du/Gene2vec) |
| Random embeddings | Negative control | Sanity check | Detects dimension-only gains | No biology | GenePT protocol |

This is a research comparison rather than a priced end-user product; pricing is
not a roadmap input. Agent Plan quota and endpoint configuration are execution
constraints, not scientific comparators.
